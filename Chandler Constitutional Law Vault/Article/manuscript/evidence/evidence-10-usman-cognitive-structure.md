Saadiq F. Usman, Making Legal Education Stick: Using Cognitive Science to Help Law Students Learn, Legal Writing Institute (2018), applies cognitive-load theory, worked examples, and spaced retrieval to law-school teaching. It is Section X's single external citation and supports X.D's claim that doctrinal courses share a learnable structure of rule, exception, and application. The source supports the learnable-structure half of that claim; it does not address the further step that this structure maps onto a small set of note types, which is the article's own inference. Section X should not present Usman as authority for the note-type mapping.
