The Prize Cases (1863) is the article's canonical iteration-loop story. Ingest pulled a Justice Grier quotation from the professor's modernized PDF and the language read clean on its face. Midpage `findInOpinion` returned zero results, which should have flagged the quotation but was logged as an OCR artifact and left alone. The corrupted quotation sat in the brief and propagated to the War Powers topic page. Several runs later, a Verify persona was specifically told not to accept zero results as an answer and switched from keyword search to `analyzeOpinion`; Midpage returned Grier's actual language and two words were wrong. The vault said "never formally declared...by its character" and Grier wrote "never solemnly declared...by its accidents." The error ran through Ingest, Lint, two Enrich passes, and Expand before Verify caught it.
