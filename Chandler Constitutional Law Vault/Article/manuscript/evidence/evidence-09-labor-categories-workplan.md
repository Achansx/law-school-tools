The workplan §3.1 enumerates four labor categories as the column-set for the cost table: ingest, prompt tuning, review, and debugging. Section IX's table architecture inherits this enumeration without inventing a fifth category and without dropping one. The cross-mapping to the system's existing phases is the article's contribution: ingest maps to the vault Ingest phase plus the Article Harvest phase; prompt tuning maps to the per-tick rubric-and-LESSONS work the author does offline plus the Article Outline and Cite work that builds each phase's prompt commitments; review maps to the per-tick scorecard reads plus the periodic LESSONS Consolidate sweep plus the Verify-phase forced-finding adversarial pass; debugging maps to the per-tick pending-issue resolution work plus the multi-tick PI lifecycle the system carries across rotations. The labor categories the workplan §3.1 names as the table's columns are the same categories the system's own phase rotation already enumerates, so the cost table reads as instrumentation of the loop rather than as a separate accounting exercise.
