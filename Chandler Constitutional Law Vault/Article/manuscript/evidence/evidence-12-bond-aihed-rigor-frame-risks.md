Melissa Bond, Hassan Khosravi, Maarten De Laat, Nina Bergdahl, Violeta Negrea, Emily Oxley, Phuong Pham, Sin Wang Chong, and George Siemens published a meta-systematic review of sixty-six AI-in-higher-education publications in volume 21, article 4 of the International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education in 2024. The review's key conclusion is the field-level need for more methodological and ethical rigor in AIHEd research. Section XI deployed Bond as the methodological-rigor anticipation frame; Section XII deploys Bond as the ethics-and-rigor anticipation frame, specifically the field-level call for honest risk disclosure that this article's risk catalogue is built to meet.

The Section XII deployment is structurally different from the Section XI deployment. At Section XI Bond's relevance is the methodological commitment the article makes (disclaim learning-outcomes claims, label the work as student-experience feedback rather than as research unless IRB so directs, frame the data slice as use-pattern and self-report rather than as outcome measurement). At Section XII Bond's relevance is the ethics-and-rigor commitment the article makes (enumerate the risks honestly, name the architectural choices that mitigate some risks and amplify others, refuse the press-release rhetoric that names a system as risk-free, place the article's contribution inside the AIHEd field's own self-critique). The two deployments are complementary anchors of the same field-level frame; Section XII inherits the URL-liveness verification from Section XI per L-034 without re-running, and the Bluebook footnote form at Section XII is a *See supra* note 8 cross-reference to Section XI's full citation plus the transfer-of-inference parenthetical the Section XII argument needs.
