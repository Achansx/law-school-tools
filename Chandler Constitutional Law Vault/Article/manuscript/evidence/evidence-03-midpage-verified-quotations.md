Every Key Quotation on every populated Case brief is followed by a Midpage line-anchor URL of the form `https://app.midpage.ai/document/<slug>-<midpage_id>?utm_source=mcp&lines=<line>` that resolves to the cited line of the indexed opinion at Midpage. The Marbury v Madison (1803) Case brief carries five Key Quotations, all linked: the "province and duty" passage at Midpage `lines=257`, the "fundamental and paramount law" passage at `lines=250`, the "essence of civil liberty" passage at `lines=156`, the "specific duty assigned by law" passage at `lines=156`, and the "superior, paramount law" passage at `lines=248`. A student who clicks any of the five lands on the verified line of Marshall's opinion at Midpage; a peer reviewer can verify every pull-quote without leaving the case page. The Commerce Clause Topic page extends the same discipline into doctrinal prose: Chief Justice Roberts's "regulation presupposes existing commercial activity" passage in the Governing Rule block is similarly linked to `https://app.midpage.ai/document/national-federation-of-independent-business-809122?utm_source=mcp&lines=276`. Section III walks this as the reviewer-grade surface that the deployed static site can carry but a chatbot answer cannot: the citation trail is on the page the student or reviewer is already reading, not retrieved by a follow-up query, and it does not depend on the LLM having generated the line number correctly.
