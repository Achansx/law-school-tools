Workplan §6 (open decisions for the professor) line 170 names copyrighted casebook material as the first of six open decisions affecting the article. The substantive directive is that none of the public-facing pages should include verbatim casebook text; the public-facing pages should work only from the professor's own slides and notes, public judicial opinions, public constitutional text, statutes, and original hypotheticals. The forward-looking discipline rule is stated; the backward-looking audit (whether any of the existing 198 deployed pages currently include casebook quotation that would need to be removed before the site is held up as a model in JLE) is an open decision pending the professor's review.

Section XII's risk catalogue must address both the forward rule and the backward audit honestly. The forward rule is a structural commitment the architecture makes: the corpus described in Section IV is composed entirely of course-issued materials (lecture decks, professor-prepared opinion PDFs, merged reading packets, practice exams, teaching guides), and none of those materials is casebook text; the vault construction in Section V also never imports casebook prose; the published pages in Section VIII derive from the vault and inherit the same discipline. The backward audit is a status the article cannot fully resolve at the manuscript-drafting altitude because the audit is a per-page inspection the professor must conduct and confirm. Section XII's prose must name the rule, name the audit as pending, and route the audit to a disclosure paragraph or to Section IX's master cost table line for the audit labor (if the audit becomes a tracked work item).

The risk extends beyond pure copyright: even if no casebook text appears verbatim on a public page, casebook organization and casebook-derived editorial decisions about which cases to include and how to sequence them carry choices made by the casebook authors and editors. The article's architecture lets the professor make these choices independently of any casebook, and Section IV's curation-actor sentence (when added per PI-061) makes the professorial curation moment visible; Section XII's risk catalogue should name the residual concern about derivative organizational choices even when no verbatim casebook text appears.
