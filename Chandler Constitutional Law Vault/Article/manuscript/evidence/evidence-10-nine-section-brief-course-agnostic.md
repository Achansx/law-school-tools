Section X.B argues that the case-brief form the vault enforces is course-agnostic, and the verified fact behind it is the template's fixed structure. Every Cases page follows the sequence PROJECT_PRIMER names the nine-section case-briefer standard: Memory Jogger, Facts, Procedural History, Judicial Votes, Holding, Analysis, Hypothetical Applications, Critique, Key Quotations, Key Points, Connections, and Sources (see evidence-05-case-brief-nine-section-standard, source-verified against the Case Brief template). The Facts, Holding, and Analysis spine is the standard law-school brief rather than a constitutional-law artifact, so a torts or contracts course briefs its cases in the same shape; only the cases and the doctrines filling the slots differ. The form generalizes; the content is what another course replaces. This is the narrower, defensible half of the schema-transfer claim and does not depend on the broader recurrence assertion PI-085 flags.
