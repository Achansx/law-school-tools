Workplan §6 (open decisions for the professor) line 173 names the public-site-as-evidence-vs-continuing-experiment question as the fourth of six open decisions. The substantive directive is that if the deployed site keeps changing during peer review, the article and the artifact will drift; the workplan recommends snapshotting the version the article describes (a tagged Git release on the source repository, or an archive.org Wayback Machine capture of the deployed URL, or both) so the manuscript and the artifact stay synchronized for the JLE reviewer who reads the article and clicks through to the deployed pages.

Section XII's risk catalogue must address the snapshot-drift question honestly because the published article will be read months or years after the manuscript is finalized, and the deployed site will continue to evolve. The article cannot truthfully describe the deployed site without telling the reader which version is being described. The Section IV snapshot date (currently held open under PI-004 as the May 15, 2026 281-page filesystem audit versus the email-progress-report 198-page snapshot) fixes the input-corpus the vault was built from; Section XII's snapshot commitment fixes the deployed pages the reader can inspect.

The risk catalogue entry covers four concrete commitments the article can make at Section XII: (1) tag the source Git repository at the snapshot date and name the tag in the prose so a future reader can check the exact source the article describes; (2) capture the deployed site at the snapshot date via the archive.org Wayback Machine and name the capture URL in the prose; (3) commit to the snapshot pair as the canonical reference for any quantitative or structural claim the article makes (page counts, search-result counts, navigation surfaces, deploy-pipeline timings); (4) name continued evolution of the deployed site after the snapshot date as the expected and intended posture, with the snapshot pair as the historical record rather than as a constraint on future development. The four commitments together let Section XII close the risk-of-drift question without committing the article to freezing the deployed site or to disclaiming the deployed site as outdated.
