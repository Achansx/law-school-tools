Melissa Bond, Hassan Khosravi, Maarten De Laat, Nina Bergdahl, Violeta Negrea, Emily Oxley, Phuong Pham, Sin Wang Chong, and George Siemens published a meta-systematic review of 66 AI-in-higher-education publications in volume 21, article 4 of the International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education in 2024. The key conclusion is the need for more methodological and ethical rigor in AIHEd research. Section XI deploys Bond as the field-level frame: the article anticipates Bond et al.'s critique by disclaiming learning-outcomes claims at the section opening and by routing the risk argument to Section XII. The deployment is JLE-idiomatic because it places the article inside the AIHEd field's own self-critique rather than treating that critique as an outside objection.
