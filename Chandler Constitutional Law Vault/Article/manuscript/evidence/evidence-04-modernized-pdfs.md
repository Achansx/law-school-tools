Thirty-seven files in the LMS export carry the modernized tag in their filenames (case examples: mcculloch_modernized.pdf, youngstown-modernized.pdf, dobbs_modernized.pdf, bruen-modernized.pdf, trump-v-us-modernized.pdf, Fulton_v_City_of_Philadelphia_Modernized.pdf). These are not slip opinions. They are professor-edited versions of older opinions that update archaic phrasing so students can read them. Modernized PDFs are one of the most pedagogically useful input types the export contained and one of the most epistemically dangerous to ingest without verification against an indexed opinion.
