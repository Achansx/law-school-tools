Ramteja Sajja, Yusuf Sermet, David M. Cwiertny, and Ibrahim Demir published "Platform-independent and curriculum-oriented intelligent assistant for higher education" in volume 20, article 42 of the International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education in 2023. The system is a working chatbot-tutor that answers student questions and is curriculum-grounded across platforms. The workplan deploys Sajja as a contrast in Section III: their system is a chatbot, the case study is a reviewed static site. Section XI uses the same paper at a different altitude: the evaluation contrast. Sajja et al.'s evaluation measures the quality of the chatbot's responses, the curriculum-grounding of its answers, and the platform-independence of its deployment, which are the right measures for a chatbot-tutor object. This article's Section XI measures use patterns and self-report on a reviewed static object, which are the right measures for a different kind of artifact. The contribution at the evaluation level is the recognition that what counts as success is artifact-dependent, and that evaluation framings must match the object being evaluated.
