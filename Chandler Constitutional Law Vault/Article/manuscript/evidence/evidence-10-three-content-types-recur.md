Section X.B claims that the shape of the schema generalizes before its content does, and the verified structural fact behind that claim is the vault's three-folder taxonomy. The case-study vault sorts every page into one of three first-class content folders, one for a decided case, one for a doctrine, and one for a class session, each backed by a canonical template and a typed frontmatter schema (see evidence-05-vault-folder-structure, source-verified against PROJECT_PRIMER). That is the base fact. The further claim, that these same three units recur in nearly every doctrinal course, is the article's argument rather than an evidenced empirical fact; it is the generalization the section advances. PI-085 already flags X.B's confident phrasing of this transfer as overclaim, since X.D concedes that a procedure or statutory course fits the assumption less cleanly. The honest harvest position is that the three-unit schema is a verified feature of the case study, and its recurrence elsewhere is asserted as a reasoned method claim that the article cannot yet demonstrate on a second instance.
