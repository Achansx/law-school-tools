The Peng et al. GraphRAG survey (arXiv:2408.08921, Aug. 15, 2024; later in ACM Transactions on Information Systems) defines the canonical Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation workflow as graph-based indexing, graph-guided retrieval, and graph-enhanced generation. The workplan flags this survey as the positioning citation for Section V: it lets the article describe the Obsidian vault as a manually maintained, professor-supervised cousin of GraphRAG, where the human-curated link graph and typed frontmatter do the retrieval work that pure semantic similarity cannot. The pedagogical claim is that a graph built by a professor with an LLM in the loop bears the same relationship to GraphRAG that a textbook bears to a scraped knowledge base: the structure is human-vouched, and that vouching is itself the pedagogical content.
