Section I.B and I.C fix the article's architectural commitment: this is not the announcement of an AI teaching assistant and not a chatbot placed in front of students, but a reviewed static website, and that choice carries the article's later argument about risk (footnote [^3], *see infra* Section XII). The workplan states the negative framing directly in its §1 'what the article is not' list, and its §7 annotated bibliography draws the same contrast against Sajja et al.'s curriculum-oriented intelligent assistant: their system is a chatbot that answers questions at runtime; ours is a reviewed static site that students browse, with the citation trail already on the page. The intro asserts the architecture; Section III walks it and Section XII argues its risk consequences.
