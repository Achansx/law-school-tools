# Rubric: Stitch

Score each criterion 1 to 5.

## 1. Terminology unity

- 1: Same concept named differently across sections without warrant.
- 3: Mostly unified; one or two terminology slippages.
- 5: "Course knowledge system" used as the umbrella term; "knowledge graph" used only as a technical mechanism; "AI tutor" never used to describe the project.

## 2. Fact consistency

- 1: Numbers, dates, or counts differ across sections.
- 3: Most facts consistent; one or two mismatches.
- 5: Page counts, dates, statistics, and counts identical across every section that references them.

## 3. Recurring sentence placement

- 1: Recurring sentence missing or used four or more times.
- 3: Used two or three times, not in the canonical spots.
- 5: Used exactly three times: introduction, Section VII / VIII seam, conclusion.

## 4. Transition quality

- 1: Section openings ignore the prior section's closing.
- 3: Some transitions earned; others abrupt.
- 5: Every section opens by acknowledging the prior section's last move and earns its own first claim.

## 5. Word count and assembly

- 1: Total under 9,000 or over 13,000 words; full-draft.md not assembled.
- 3: Total within 9,000 to 13,000; assembled but with stale sections.
- 5: Total within 10,000 to 12,000; full-draft.md assembled from current polished/ files; total_words and last_stitched_at updated in state.
