# Rubric: Polish

Score each criterion 1 to 5.

## 1. Mechanical hygiene

- 1: Em dashes or straight ASCII quotes present in published prose.
- 3: Most removed; some remain.
- 5: Zero em dashes; zero straight quotes. All quotation marks curly.

## 2. Voice consistency

- 1: Voice slips into tech-enthusiast, marketing, or LinkedIn register.
- 3: Voice mostly consistent; one or two slips per page.
- 5: Practitioner-scholarly throughout. Could be read aloud at an AALS panel.

## 3. Sentence tightness

- 1: Many sentences over 35 words without justification; passive voice without need.
- 3: Some long sentences; some passive voice.
- 5: One idea per sentence. Active voice unless the actor is unknowable. No sentence longer than 35 words unless deliberate.

## 4. First-person discipline

- 1: First-person used in analytic passages without warrant.
- 3: First-person used inconsistently.
- 5: First-person reserved for narrative passages about the project; third-person elsewhere.

## 5. Section coherence

- 1: Section reads as a list of paragraphs.
- 3: Paragraphs flow; section has a beginning and an end.
- 5: Section opens with its thesis sentence, develops it, and closes by setting up the next section.
