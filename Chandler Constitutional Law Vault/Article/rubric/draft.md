# Rubric: Draft

Score each criterion 1 to 5.

## 1. Argument structure

- 1: Section's argument is unclear; could be in any order.
- 3: Section has an argument; transitions weak.
- 5: Section has a thesis sentence, supporting moves, and a closing that earns the next section.

## 2. Evidence use

- 1: Evidence cards referenced ignored.
- 3: Most evidence used; some claims unsupported.
- 5: Every evidence card relevant to the section is used or explicitly deferred.

## 3. Voice

- 1: Tech-enthusiast, marketing, or product-announcement register.
- 3: Voice mixed; some sentences in voice, some out.
- 5: Practitioner-scholarly throughout. Could appear in JLE without recasting.

## 4. Fabrication discipline

- 1: At least one claim, statistic, quotation, or citation appears fabricated or unverifiable.
- 3: No outright fabrication but some claims rest on `[TODO]` markers that should have triggered Harvest.
- 5: Zero fabrication. Every claim has a citation placeholder pointing to an evidence card or a verifiable external source.

## 5. Word budget compliance

- 1: Section exceeds budget by more than 25 percent or is under 50 percent of target.
- 3: Within 15 to 25 percent of target.
- 5: Within plus or minus 10 percent of target.
