# Rubric: Verify

Score each criterion 1 to 5.

## 1. Persona coverage

- 1: Only one or two personas read the draft.
- 3: All three personas read but findings shallow.
- 5: All three personas read independently; each returned at least one finding.

## 2. Forced-finding compliance

- 1: A persona returned no findings.
- 3: All personas returned findings but at least one was a token P2.
- 5: Every persona returned at least one substantive finding (P0, P1, or a meaningful P2 with explanation).

## 3. Finding specificity

- 1: Findings name no section, paragraph, or claim.
- 3: Findings reference sections but not specific text.
- 5: Every finding names section, paragraph, and the specific text or claim at issue.

## 4. Suggested-fix quality

- 1: No suggested fixes; or fixes that say "rewrite."
- 3: Suggested fixes present but vague.
- 5: Every finding includes a one- or two-sentence concrete fix that the next Polish or Draft run can execute.

## 5. State routing

- 1: P0 and P1 findings not routed back into per-section status.
- 3: Some findings routed; some left as dangling.
- 5: Every P0 and P1 finding routes the affected section's status back to `needs_polish` or `needs_draft`; verify-findings.md updated with the routing.
