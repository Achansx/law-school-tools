# Rubric: Outline

Score each criterion 1 to 5.

## 1. Structural fidelity

- 1: Outline does not match the section's role per PROJECT_PRIMER.
- 3: Outline matches the section but not the article's overall arc.
- 5: Outline matches both the section's role and the article's voice and arc.

## 2. Evidence binding

- 1: Bullets lack evidence pointers.
- 3: Most bullets have evidence pointers; some are vague.
- 5: Every bullet has a specific `[evidence: evidence-NN-slug]` pointer.

## 3. Word budgeting

- 1: No word budgets per subsection.
- 3: Budgets present; sum off by more than 15 percent of section target.
- 5: Budgets present; sum within 10 percent of section target.

## 4. Argument granularity

- 1: Bullets are vague topic headings.
- 3: Bullets state what will be argued but not how.
- 5: Bullets state the claim, the evidence, and the inferential move.

## 5. Open question discipline

- 1: No open-questions block; gaps unmentioned.
- 3: Block present but incomplete.
- 5: Block explicitly lists every claim that cannot yet be supported, with what would be needed to close the gap.
